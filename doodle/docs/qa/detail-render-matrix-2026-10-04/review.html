Method and limits

The scene, model (gpt-image-1-mini), 1024 × 1024 size and one-image request stay the same. Within each detail level, only rendering quality changes. The app's actual Simple, Medium and Detailed prompts are used unchanged.

Each cell is one independent generation. Composition varies naturally. Times are measured provider response times. Cost estimates use published image-output prices plus reported text-input tokens, before invoice reconciliation.

Release appearance applies the unchanged production paper-correction helper. Guarded exclusions preserve the original instead. No marketing edits, automatic grading, regeneration or production changes. This experiment is separate from live-route, Play-installed Integrity and credit verification.
