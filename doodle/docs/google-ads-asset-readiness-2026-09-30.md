## Custom assets held

The two generated lifestyle mockups are 1024 x 1536 PNGs, below 5 MB each. Their 2:3 shape is outside Google's listed custom-image shapes of 1:1, 1.91:1 and 4:5. They require intentional cropping or recomposition and visual review before use as custom image assets. They are illustrative mockups, not customer photographs. The feature graphic's 1024 x 500 store format is also not a substitute for a custom 1.91:1 upload.

Existing demo videos end with the website destination. They are held for this Android install test pending review of the final CTA and current UI. A custom video is optional, so this need not delay initial preparation.
